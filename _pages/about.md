I am a PhD student in Astrophysics at the University of Vienna.

My research explores the journey of the Solar System through the Milky Way and investigates how the different Galactic environments it encounters may have left traces in Earth's geological records.

Beyond research, I am interested in science communication, sustainability, and human rights, and I actively contribute to a range of related projects.
