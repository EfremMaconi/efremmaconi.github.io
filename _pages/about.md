I am a PhD student at the Department of Astrophysics at the University of Vienna.

My research explores the journey of the Solar System through the Milky Way and aims to characterize the various environments the Sun has encountered along its orbit. I investigate passages through gas clouds and nearby supernovae that can leave their radioactive fingerprints in geological and lunar records.

Beyond research, I am interested in science communication, sustainability, and human rights, and I actively contribute to a range of related projects.
